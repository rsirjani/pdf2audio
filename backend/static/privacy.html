Privacy Policy

Effective date: (to be set on launch)

What we collect

  • Your email address, as authenticated by Cloudflare Access when you sign in.
  • PDFs you upload, plus the parsed structured-text and TTS audio derived from them. Each upload is stored under your user identifier on the operator's NAS.
  • Usage counts: number of PDFs you've uploaded today, total storage, for the purpose of enforcing rate limits.
  • Request logs with timestamps, IPs, and user-agent strings, kept by Cloudflare for security and abuse-prevention.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not store passwords (Cloudflare handles auth, including one-time PINs).
  • We do not run third-party analytics or ad trackers.
  • We do not share your PDFs, parsed text, or audio with anyone.

How we use your data

  • To provide the Service: parse, narrate, and serve back your own documents.
  • To enforce per-user rate limits.
  • To respond to support requests, abuse reports, or legal requests.

Where data is stored

Your PDFs, parsed JSON, and audio files are stored on the operator's personal NAS at a private residence in Canada. Backups, if any, are kept on the same hardware. No cloud-storage providers are used.

Tips

Ko-fi processes optional tips and has its own privacy policy. The operator only sees aggregate amounts and your displayed name + message if you provided one.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing ramtin.sirjani@gmail.com. Deletion is typically processed within 14 days.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, do not use the Service.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may change. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

Contact
